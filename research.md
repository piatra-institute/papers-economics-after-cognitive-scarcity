## Seed corrections and departures

The seed proposed a large architecture with environmental dynamics, endogenous
constitutional change and a decentralised investment equilibrium, and reported a
twelve-period optimisation with synthetic coefficients passing 791 assertions.
None of that is used. Synthetic coefficients over twelve abstract periods produce
numbers with no interpretation, and the seed said so itself. The three benchmarks
here were chosen because each has an exact solution or a closed-form limit that
can be stated without inventing a calibration.

The seed's headline allocation table is reproduced exactly and independently:
9.000 against 0.111 at 81 to 1, 5.000 against 0.556 at 9 to 1, and 1.000 each.
The tenfold-output experiment is also reproduced, including that housing
quantities do not move.

The central result of this paper, the limit $\delta/(a+\delta)$ and the
cancellation of the arrival rate, is not in the seed. Neither is the fallback
plane, the containment crossing, or the case-mix decomposition; the seed
identified the case-mix confound as a criticism worth making and did not
quantify it.

## Open source questions

Whether any published estimate exists of the rate at which capability overtakes
a newly created task, which is the parameter $a$ in the limit, was not
established; no source consulted reports one, and the paper treats it as a
quantity to be measured rather than assumed. The same holds for task turnover.
